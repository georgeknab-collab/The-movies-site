THE COMPANY

WHAT IS GEORGE'S UNIVERSE?

George's Universe is a creative technology company built around entertainment, games, movies, websites, and new ideas. The goal is to create a connected universe of projects that people can watch, play, explore, and enjoy.

🎬

Movies

Original movies and entertainment projects designed to become part of the George's Universe world.

🎮

Games

Browser games, experiments, and interactive experiences that give visitors something to play.

💻

Technology

Websites, tools, CGI experiments, AI projects, and other technology ideas.

THE VISION

ONE UNIVERSE.
MANY STORIES.

George's Universe isn't meant to be just one website.

The long-term idea is to create a collection of movies, games, characters, stories, technology, and experiences that can all grow together.

Every new project can become another part of the Universe.
